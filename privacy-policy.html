Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026 • WASA Kasur Enterprise System

This Privacy Policy describes how WASA Kasur (Water & Sanitation Agency, Kasur – Government of Punjab) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the WASA Kasur Digital Management System (the “App”).

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Name, phone number, CNIC, designation, and role (Officer / Admin).
  • Field Data: Consumer records, recovery amounts, survey responses, GPS location, photos, signatures, and voice notes submitted by officers.
  • Device Data: Device type, browser, network status (for offline sync), and app version.
  • Communication Data: In-app messages and call metadata (for audit purposes). Call recordings are stored securely and accessible only to authorized Super Administrators.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate the official water utility management system of WASA Kasur.
  • To enable field officers to record recoveries, surveys, and consumer updates.
  • To generate reports, dashboards, and audit trails for management.
  • To send official notifications (approvals, assignments, alerts).
  • To provide secure in-app communication between authorized staff.

3. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored on Google Firebase (secure cloud infrastructure) and Cloudinary (media files). Access is strictly role-based. Passwords are encrypted. Super Admin accounts are invisible in normal user lists. Offline data on devices is encrypted and automatically synced when internet is available.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for marketing. Data is used only for official WASA Kasur operations and may be shared with authorized government auditors when legally required.

5. Officer & Admin Responsibilities

  • Officers must not share login credentials.
  • Photos and consumer data must be captured only for official work.
  • Admins must protect access to the admin panel and hidden settings.

6. Your Rights

Authorized administrators can request correction or deletion of records through the official admin panel. For any privacy-related request, contact the system administrator.

7. Children’s Privacy

This App is intended only for authorized WASA staff and is not directed at children under 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect changes. Continued use of the App means you accept the updated policy.
