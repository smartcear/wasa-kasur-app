Privacy Policy — WASA Kasur Digital Management System

Last updated: 2026

This policy describes information processed by the WASA Kasur digital management system for authorized water-utility operations.

Information

  • Officer name, phone, CNIC, role and zone information.
  • Consumer records including consumer number, name, address, category, contact and service information.
  • Official field activities, GPS/location, photos, videos, voice notes and form submissions.
  • Official PDF bills and their consumer-number associations.
  • Notifications, audit records, permissions and authorized communication data.

Purpose

Information is processed for official operations, consumer services, field verification, reporting, auditing, communication, document management and security.

Offline Storage

Some authorized data may be temporarily stored in browser/device storage to support offline operation and later synchronization.

Cloud Services

The system uses Google Firebase for configured authentication/database/storage services and may use Cloudinary for authorized media workflows.

Access Control

Access to consumers, reports, bills, exports and administration is controlled by roles and permissions and should be enforced by backend security rules.

Review required: This is an application privacy-policy template and should be approved by the relevant WASA/legal authority before official publication.

Contact

Contact the authorized WASA Kasur administration for privacy or data-access questions.
